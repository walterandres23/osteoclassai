OsteoClassAI · Knee osteoarthritis grading

Kellgren-Lawrence grade from a knee X-ray, computed entirely in your browser: the image never leaves your device. A detector finds each knee and crops it at the scale the classifier was trained on; then ConvNeXt-Tiny (ordinal loss, robustness augmentation) grades it. Patient-disjoint test set: QWK 0.867, AUC 0.950 for KL ≥ 2. Code and evaluation
